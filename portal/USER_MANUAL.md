# User Manual

## Login

1. Open the portal URL.
2. Enter your username/email and password.
3. You will be redirected to your dashboard based on role.

## Dashboard

- View total members, payment status, overdue alerts, and recent activity.
- Executives see only their assigned members.

## Members

- Search by name, member ID, email, or phone.
- Filter by status.
- Open a member profile for details, fees, and documents.

## Executive Assignment

- Admin roles can assign members to executives.
- Each executive can have a maximum of 10 members.
- The system blocks assignments beyond this limit.

## Documents

- Upload member documents (Emirates ID, passport, photo, others).
- Max file size per document: 5MB.
- View/download files from the member row actions.
- Track expiry date for IDs/passports where applicable.

## Fees

- Create annual fees per member and fee year.
- Track statuses: unpaid, partial, paid, overdue.
- Mark payments with method and transaction reference.

## WhatsApp

- Open reminder messages directly in WhatsApp.
- Message logs are recorded for audit/history.
- Executives can access only assigned member reminders.

## Best Practices

- Keep member contacts and WhatsApp numbers updated.
- Upload expiry-prone documents with expiry date.
- Record payment references to simplify reconciliation.
